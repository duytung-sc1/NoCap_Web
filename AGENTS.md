# Typography — mandatory

All text is sans-serif, including brand, headings, alerts, administration, reading, stealth and PDF exports. Vietnamese uses Be Vietnam Pro; English uses Plus Jakarta Sans. Keep separate families/assets and resolve content language independently of the interface. Never apply an English font to Vietnamese and rely on fallback. Normalize legacy reader fonts to language-based sans and do not restore serif/mono/custom choices. Preserve original PDF/image files. Validate both locales and Vietnamese diacritics.
